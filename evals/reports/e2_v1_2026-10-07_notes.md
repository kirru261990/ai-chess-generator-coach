# Notes on the E2 run of 2026-10-07 (read before quoting any number)

Written by Claude after reading every wrong claim in the `verified` texts. **These are one reader's judgements, not a human
expert's, and the extractor was not checked by a person.** The frozen set, prompts and the numbers in `e2_v1_2026-10-07.md` are
unchanged; this file only explains them.

## The result as measured
`verified` scored 143 of 154 checkable claims correct (93%, 95% interval 88% to 96%). **The 98% target in the spec was not met as
measured.** Nothing below changes that.

## What the 11 wrong claims in the `verified` texts look like
| Item | Claim the extractor wrote | My reading |
|---|---|---|
| e2-015 | `Bxd5+` captures, position after | **Extractor mislabel.** The text says the capture comes two moves later in the engine line; the extractor placed it right after the played move |
| e2-021 | line `Qxd2+ exd2+ Kxd2` is legal | **Real error that got through.** The text names a move sequence in an order that is not legal; each move was in the evidence, but the order was never checked |
| e2-035 | band `losing`; Bg6 can be taken | **Extractor over-reading.** The text says "blunder, cost 6.1 pawns" and "left it there"; it never says losing. The position after the move is about equal (-0.3) |
| e2-039 | piece on f6 can be taken | **Extractor mislabel.** "You took a free piece on f6" is about the piece the player captured |
| e2-040 | band `worse`; line ending `g7` | **Over-reading** (band) and **loose line** (the text says the pawn "can keep marching"; the extractor made it the next move) |
| e2-041 | band `losing` | **Extractor over-reading.** The text never says losing (position -0.8) |
| e2-042 | line `Ne4+ Kc2 Rfc8+ Nf2+` is legal | **Real error that got through.** It skips White's reply between `Rfc8+` and `Nf2+` |
| e2-050 | free piece on d4; band `losing` | **Over-reading.** The text says the rook "could capture" the bishop, not that it was free; and never says losing |

So 7 of the 11 wrong claims are `eval_band` or `free_piece` readings the text does not literally make, 2 more are position
mislabels, and **2 or 3 are real**: move sequences in the prose whose order is not legal. The same extractor noise affects
`raw` and `grounded`, so their error counts are also overstated by some unknown amount.

## What the run does support (with the limits above)
- **Evidence helps a lot.** Texts with at least one wrong claim: `raw` 27 of 50, `grounded` 9 of 50. Correct rate 80% to 93%.
  Unverifiable statements (things nobody can check): `raw` 243, `grounded` 71. Even with extractor noise, the direction is clear.
- **The verifier changed little here.** `grounded` 9 texts with a wrong claim, `verified` 8. It repaired 9 of 50 first drafts, fell
  back to plain facts once, and the unverifiable count only dropped from 71 to 68. The noise in the measurement is larger than
  the gate's measured effect, so this run cannot say how much the gate is worth.
- **Unverifiable statements remain high** (68 in `verified`, about 1.4 per text): the coach still says things the verifier cannot
  check (forks, attacks, plans, "keeps the initiative"). Rule 2 is only partly met by claims-based checking.

## Real findings to act on
1. **Prose move sequences are not verified** (e2-021, e2-042). The prose guard checks that each named move is in the evidence, not
   that a stated sequence is legal in that order. Needs a sequence check in the verifier (v2): any run of two or more moves in the
   text must be a legal line from the position it belongs to.
2. **The extractor over-reads.** It turns "blunder" into a `losing` band and "could capture" into a free-piece claim. `extract_v2`
   should be told never to infer an evaluation band or "free" unless the text uses such a word. A person should read a sample of
   extractions before the next published run.
3. **Unverifiable statements**: decide whether the coach prompt should forbid them (`why_v2`) or the verifier should learn more
   claim types (forks, pins, checks).
4. The raw baseline is the same Claude model, not a GPT model (ADR 0003, decision 1); a GPT raw config is still open.

Any fix is a new version (`verifier` v2, `why_v2`, `extract_v2`, `e2_v2` if the set changes) and a new run, never an edit to
the frozen files.
