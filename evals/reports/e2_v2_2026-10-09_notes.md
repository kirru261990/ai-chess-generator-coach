# Notes on the E2 v2 run of 2026-10-09 (read before quoting any number)

Written by Claude. The set (`e2_v2`, 50 positions never used for tuning), the prompts and the verifier version were frozen before
this run (`evals/sets/e2_v2/FROZEN.md`); the run was started once, interrupted once by an empty Anthropic balance (its results
were lost and nothing was changed afterwards), and re-run from scratch. Design and limits: ADR 0003 and ADR 0004.

## The result as measured
| Config | Checkable claims correct | Rate (95% interval) | Texts with a wrong claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw (no tools, no evidence) | 125 / 160 | 78% (71% to 84%) | 25 of 50 |
| grounded (first draft, no verifier) | 119 / 123 | 97% (92% to 99%) | 4 of 50 |
| verified (what the player sees) | **110 / 110** | **100% (97% to 100%)** | **0 of 50** |

**Against the spec's target of at least 98% for `verified`: met as a point estimate, not established.** The lower end of the interval
is 97%, so with this many claims even a perfect score cannot show 98% with confidence. Two `verified` texts and one `grounded`
text could not be scored (claim extraction failed twice); they are not counted as correct, so 48 of the 50 `verified` texts are
behind the 110 claims.

## What the numbers support
- **Evidence helps a lot.** Giving the model the engine and detector evidence took the share of texts with a wrong claim from
  25 of 50 to 4 of 50, and the claim rate from 78% to 97%.
- **The verifier removed every remaining wrong claim in this run.** The 4 wrong claims in the first drafts (a piece wrongly said to
  be takeable on h5 and on a5, and two captures that are not legal) were all caught: 12 of 50 drafts were repaired and none fell back
  to plain facts. These are rules-based checks (legality and the hanging-piece detector), which is the part of the verifier least
  open to the objection below.
- **Compared with v1:** 93% before, 100% now, but on a different set, with a different extractor and a changed prompt and verifier. The
  separation done on the v1 positions (ADR 0004) suggests the extractor alone is worth about 2 points. This run does not show how
  much each change contributed.

## What the numbers do not support
- **Not an independent check.** The scorer reuses the verifier's claim checks (and the `hanging_own` / `missed_free` detectors),
  at a higher engine depth. A `verified` text is graded by the logic that gated it. The honest reading is "the explanations
  contain no claim the verifier's own checks can refute", not "they are all true".
- **Many statements are still unverifiable: 30 in the `verified` texts (about 0.6 per text).** Examples: "it gives check", "forces
  White to respond", "Black must recapture with the pawn", "attacks the bishop on h5", and "none of your pieces was left to be
  taken" (a fact the harness itself got from the detector, but the extractor cannot express it as a claim, so it counts as
  unverifiable although it is backed). Rule 2 is met for the claims that can be expressed and silent about the rest.
- **Extractor and set limits are as in ADR 0003:** the extractor is the same model as the coach, no person has read a sample of its
  output, the items are puzzle-derived and engine-defined, and one run with a non-deterministic model and engine will differ if
  repeated. The raw baseline is the same Claude model, not a GPT model.
- **The texts can be correct and still poor teaching** ("That was a blunder, costing 5.4 pawns. The better move was pawn to h6."). E2
  measures correctness only.

## Cost and spend
About 2.05 dollars for this run (336k input and 138k output tokens), recorded by the usage ledger (T36); the month's recorded spend after the
run was 8.85 of the 10 dollar budget (including a 6.80 estimate for spend before the ledger existed).

## To act on
1. Add the cheap missing claim types so fewer statements are unverifiable: `gives_check`, and a check that a stated forced reply is
   the only legal move; let the extractor express harness-provided facts ("none of your pieces was left to be taken").
2. A person reads a sample of 15 to 20 extractions (did the extractor transcribe the text faithfully?) before this number is quoted outside the repo.
3. A GPT raw baseline (needs an OpenAI key and its own dependency record).
4. Any change from here is a new version (`why_v3`, `verifier` v3, `e2_v3`) and a new run on a new set, never an edit to the frozen files.
